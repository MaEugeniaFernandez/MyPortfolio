title: Evaluation of Individual Project vs Team Project
---

The Unit 6 group project proposed an AI-assisted logical database to help a healthcare organisation analyse readmissions and resource use. Supported by ChatGPT and Gemini on Google Colab, our team designed the database, data processing workflows, and an intelligent dashboard, receiving highly positive tutor feedback.

The Unit 11 final project executed the proposal from Unit 6. Working independently, I used Gemini for Python code generation, schema detection, and data cleaning, ultimately building an interactive, filtered dashboard in Tableau Public to visualise the validated data.

A major difference between the projects was transitioning from a team environment to working solo. While our group produced excellent work, dividing the initial workload led to highly siloed tasks. Consequently, building the final project independently was challenging, as I lacked hands-on experience with the sections my teammates had completed.

The second key difference was the depth of the insights obtained. The Unit 6 design phase provided only a superficial glance at the data. In contrast, the Unit 11 actual build revealed substantial data-quality issues that made the raw data unreliable for operational decisions. This highlighted a critical lesson: without rigorous human intervention, significant data flaws can easily hide behind convincing AI-generated outputs.

In retrospect, transitioning from a collaborative design to an independent build demonstrated that hands-on experience and human oversight are essential to uncover deep data-quality issues that AI alone might overlook.
