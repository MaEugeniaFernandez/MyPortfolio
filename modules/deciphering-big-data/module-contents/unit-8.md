The collaborative learning discussion was aimed at comparing the rules of GDPR regarding securing personal data with the UK’s ICO rules on the same topic. I used directly the text from the EU Regulation (EU) 2016/679 for this comparison as I live and work in the EU, and I focused specifically on the application of these regulations in big data wrangling environments, and in the structural exemptions present in the UK Data Protection Act of 2018.

The collaborative learning discussion covers module outcome 1 of the course.

As an information professional living and working in the EU, and specifically in a Higher Education institution, I am very aware of GDPR regulations and their application in my workplace. Anyway, this unit was a nice refresher of the core principles related to data protection and of the existing frameworks and standards and their applications within the different industry sectors. The initial post from the collaborative learning discussion was also a nice opportunity to dive a bit deeper into the regulations, so I am looking forward to continuing with this discussion to learn further on compliance and data protection regulations.
