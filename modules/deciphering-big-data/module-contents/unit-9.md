In unit 9 we continued with the collaborative learning discussion about comparing compliance laws in regard to the security of personal data (specifically GDPR and UK ICO’s rules and regulations). The task this week was to respond to at least two initial posts from peers to continue developing the conversation on the topic. I replied to two of my peers’ posts, both which covered the topic of security risks of data during the phase of data wrangling.

This unit had plenty of theoretical contents related to database design, database management systems, and database security issues, which were really interesting to learn in order to understand more about what is behind the databases we so often interact with. The reading about big data architectures was also quite insightful since this was a new topic for me. Lastly, continuing with the collaborative learning discussion by replying to fellow classmates’ posts was also a very nice exercise where I learned more about data regulation and compliance laws in different countries, and specifically about a few very interesting incidents that happened in the UK around this topic that I was not aware of as I don’t live there.
