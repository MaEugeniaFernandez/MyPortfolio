# Professional Skills Matrix and Action Plan

This section presents my Professional Skills Matrix and Personal Development Plan (PDP) for the Deciphering Big Data module. A PDP is a structured cycle of assessing my skills, reflecting on them, setting goals, acting on them and reviewing progress. Clegg and Bradley (2006) describe three models of PDP in higher education: professional, employment and academic. This plan follows mainly the employment and professional models, because it connects what I learned in the module with the skills that data-related roles require.

The skills chosen to evaluate come from the module’s four learning outcomes (LO1–LO4). I checked them against De Mauro *et al.*’s (2018) classification of the skill sets that employers ask for in Big Data job postings. Research shows that people’s self-assessments of their own skills are often inaccurate (Dunning, Heath and Suls, 2004). For that reason, every rating below is linked to an artefact, an activity or tutor feedback in this e-portfolio.

I will review this matrix at the end of each future module and update the “Now” column and the status of each goal. Self-ratings can be biased (Dunning, Heath and Suls, 2004), so I will check them against tutor feedback, grades and peer comments, and not only against how confident I feel. This follows Zimmerman’s (2002) description of self-regulated learning as a repeated cycle of planning, performance and self-reflection.

**My starting point:** I am an information professional with a background in Library and Information Sciences and information behaviour research. I do not have an IT background, so almost all the technical content in this module was new to me.
