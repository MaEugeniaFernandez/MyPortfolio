This unit introduced big data and the technologies underpinning modern data management by presenting the concept, features and characteristics of big data, and by exploring the 4Vs of big data (volume, velocity, variety and veracity) and the methodologies, tools and strategies organisations use to work with it, including the security obligations that come with holding data at scale.

This collaborative discussion explored data collection in the context of the Internet of Things (IoT), focusing on the challenges, security issues and risks, limitations, and opportunities in data wrangling. For my initial post in the discussion, I focused on the human factor of the data cleaning process highlighted by Huxley (2020), and the issue of its scalability when working with big data.

This activity covered the module’s learning outcomes 1 and 2.

All the concepts introduced in this unit were fairly new to me, so I was happy to find all the resources available (lecturecast, live session recording and readings) beginner-friendly enough for me to start diving into the world of big data with a strong theoretical base.
